# Code of conduct

Contingram welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience.
Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully. Be clear about uncertainty, credit
others' work, and help make technical discussion accessible.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and
deliberate disclosure of private information are unacceptable. Criticism of
code or a technical claim is welcome; attacks on a person are not.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
official project spaces. The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content,
limit participation, or ban participants, considering severity, context, and
repeated behavior. Enforcement should be proportionate and explain the reason
without exposing private information.

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer through the
[project profile](https://github.com/Nima0101). If no private contact route is
available, request one in an issue without naming affected people or posting
sensitive details. Reports are handled with care, but the project cannot
promise confidentiality against platform requirements or legal obligations.
GitHub's platform reporting tools remain available for abuse.
